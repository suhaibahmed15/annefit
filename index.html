How I build your plan

Nothing gets written until I know how you train and how you live.

Most programs fail because they treat every body the same. Before I write a single set, I look at how you move, what your week actually looks like, how well you recover, and what you have already tried. Then the plan gets built around that — and adjusted as you go.

About Coach Anne

I coach the way I wish someone had coached me.

I have spent twenty years around strength and competitive sport, first as a semi-professional handball player and then coaching. In that time I have watched a lot of women work extremely hard for very little in return, not because they lacked discipline, but because nobody gave them a structure worth following.

In March 2025 I ruptured my ACL and tore my meniscus in several places. Rebuilding took surgery, patience, and every principle I teach. It changed how I coach: I understand from the inside what it takes to trust your own body again, and why rushing it never works.
