Frequently Asked Questions:

How Does Online Coaching With Anneloes Work?
Everything runs through a coaching app. You get a personalised training and nutrition plan, video exercise guidance, weekly check-ins and direct contact with me when you need it. We adjust as you progress, so the plan always fits your life, not the other way around.
Do You Coach Men As Well As Women?
My coaching is built around women, but I also work with men who want structured strength, muscle or performance coaching. If you are committed, you are welcome.
Do I Need A Gym Or Special Equipment?
Programs can be custom-tailored to full commercial gyms, home setups, or minimal equipment depending on what you have access to for optimal progressive overload.
What Is The Difference Between Strong Foundation, Full Strength And Kickstart?
Each tier offers distinct levels of direct communication, video audit frequency, and customization granularity, tailored precisely to your timeline and goals.
I Already Train But Feel Stuck. Can You Help?
Absolutely! Plateaus are usually caused by a lack of tracking metrics or structural recovery imbalances. We will audit your current routine and break through immediately.
What Is The Peach Plan?
The Peach Plan is our specialized lower-body hypertrophy block designed for targeted glute development, structural posture, and progressive aesthetic shaping.
